How to choose a HWID spoofer

Two of the nine products above are spoofers on their own: the 420 Spoofer and the redENGINE Spoofer. The other seven come with a cheat, and the spoofer is part of the same plan. If you only need to get past a hardware ban, a standalone spoofer is enough; if you want to play with a cheat as well, a bundle covers both in one purchase.

They also differ in how long the spoof lasts. The Eulen Cheats + Spoofer, the redENGINE Spoofer, and the Susano Cheats + Spoofer spoof for one session only: after every reboot you run the spoofer again before you play. The Keyser Cheat + Spoofer carries a permanent built-in spoofer. With the 420 Spoofer you restart the PC after spoofing, and the new identifiers apply after that restart.

The requirements differ, so check your PC against the product page before you buy. The redENGINE Spoofer is made for 64-bit Windows 10; on Windows 11 it only runs on older builds. The Seryx Cheat + Spoofer needs TPM switched off in the BIOS and a Windows disk in GPT format, and the first start reboots the PC once. The Susano Cheats + Spoofer needs TPM 2.0 active, CPU virtualisation switched on in the BIOS and Hyper-V switched off. Those two ask for opposite TPM settings. The Eulen Cheats + Spoofer and the redENGINE Spoofer do not run in a virtual machine or on a cloud PC such as Shadow.

No spoofer prevents the next ban: it gets a banned machine back in, and a server can still ban the new identity for what you do next. The redENGINE Spoofer and the Susano Cheats + Spoofer expect a fresh Rockstar account on top. The Eulen Cheats + Spoofer needs a Social Club account that owns GTA V, since a fresh free account is not enough; it does not cover newer ban methods, and on a big server with its own anti-cheat you also need a new IP and new Steam, Social Club and Discord accounts.

Run the spoofer before you start the game, never while it is open or while a ban message is on screen. For the 420 Spoofer and the Susano Cheats + Spoofer, you also sign in to Windows with a local account instead of a Microsoft account and uninstall the Xbox apps, which keep sending identifiers of their own. Skipping those clean-up steps is the usual reason for being banned again straight away.

Eight of the nine product pages list a hardware ID reset from your account, for the day you change your PC.

What a HWID spoofer changes

A ban for cheating is rarely just a ban on the account. The anti-cheat also writes down what identifies the machine you played on: the serial numbers of your disks, your network adapter's MAC address, the identifiers of your processor and graphics card. Make a new account on the same PC and the ban is waiting for you there.

A HWID spoofer changes what the game reads in those places, so the hardware ban stops following you. It does not touch the account itself — a banned Rockstar, Steam or Discord account stays banned, and for that you need a fresh account.

Questions about spoofers

What is a HWID spoofer?
HWID stands for hardware ID. A game that bans you for cheating does not only ban the account - it also writes down what identifies the machine you played on, such as your disk serial numbers, your network adapter's MAC address and the identifiers of your processor and graphics card. A HWID spoofer changes what the game reads in those places, so a hardware ban stops following you to the next account. Some stores call the same product a HWID changer.
Does a spoofer lift an account ban?
No. A spoofer only touches the hardware identifiers. A banned Rockstar, Steam or Discord account stays banned, and for that you need a fresh account rather than a spoofer.
Do I need to buy the spoofer separately from the cheat?
That depends on the product. Some of the products listed above are standalone spoofers; the others load the spoofer from the same loader as the cheat, with nothing extra to buy. Each product page states which of the two it is.
