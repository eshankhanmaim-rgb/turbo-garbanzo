  • Can I move my license to a new PC?

    Yes. Open an HWID Reset ticket on Discord with your product name, license key and proof of purchase, and we move it for you.

  • Can I use one key on two PCs?

    No. One key is for one person on one machine. Sharing it, or trying to work around that, ends the license — no exceptions.

  • What is an HWID spoofer and do I need one?

    A hardware ban locks out your PC, not your account — a spoofer changes the fingerprint the game reads, so you get back in. You need one only after such a ban.

    Some of our products include a spoofer for their game; the rest are in the shop. Note that a spoofed PC still needs a fresh game account.

    Read the HWID spoofer guide

  • The loader will not start — what now?

    Three things fix almost every case: turn your antivirus off (Windows Defender completely, including real-time protection and tamper protection), right-click the file and run it as administrator, and install the Visual C++ and DirectX runtimes.

    Still closing? Restart the PC first — a loader that was blocked once stays blocked until the next boot. The full list is under Downloads.

  • Do I need to disable my antivirus?

    Yes. Antivirus programs can block the loader and the spoofer, so turn your antivirus off before you run them. For Windows Defender, turn it off completely, including real-time protection and tamper protection: Windows Security → Virus & threat protection → Manage settings.

    Defender flags this kind of software by category, not because it found something.

  • My key shows as invalid — what now?

    Check for a missing character first, then look it up under Redeem a key in your account: it tells you whether the key is on your account, on someone else's, or unknown to us.

    If it is yours and still refused, open a ticket in Discord with your order number.
